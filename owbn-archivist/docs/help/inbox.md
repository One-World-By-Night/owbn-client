# Archivist Inbox

Your requests in one list: the ones waiting on you, the ones you filed, and the ones you are watching.

## Who can use this

Anyone signed in.

## How to get there

- **On the website:** the Archivist Inbox page, or **View Inbox** on the [Archivist Dashboard](dashboard.md).
- **In wp-admin:** Archivist → Workspace → **Inbox** tab.

## The screen

- **The tabs:**
  - **Assigned to Me** - requests at a step you have, by name or by role. This is your work.
  - **My Submissions** - the last 20 requests you filed, wherever they are.
  - **Watching** - requests you follow with **Watch**.
- **All Domains** and **All Statuses** - narrow the list to one domain or one status.
- **Search entries…** - narrows the list as you type.
- **The table** - **Entry** (who filed it, the domain and the number), **Domain**, **Step**, **Status**, **Date** and **View**, which opens [A Request's Page](entry.md).
- **In wp-admin**, the Workspace's **Inbox** tab shows the same requests as three lists, one under the other: those waiting on you, **Watched Entries** and **My Entries**, with a domain filter on top.

## Common tasks

### Work through what is waiting on you

1. Open **Assigned to Me**.
2. Click **View** on a request, act on it, and come back for the next.

### Check on a request you filed

1. Open **My Submissions** and find it; **Step** and **Status** show where it stands.

## Things to know

- You get an email when a request reaches your step, and when a request you watch moves on or finishes. Choose one email a day instead on the [Archivist Dashboard](dashboard.md).
- A request you file shows under My Submissions at once.
- For the Archivist, Assigned to Me holds every open request, at any step.
- Watching a request puts it under Watching; click **Watching** on its page to stop.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Please log in to access your inbox."** Sign in first.
- **"No entries found."** Nothing in this tab yet.
- **"No entries match your filters."** Set the filters back to All Domains and All Statuses, and clear the search.
- **A request I should review isn't under Assigned to Me.** It isn't at your step yet, or your account doesn't hold the role the step needs. Its page's **Assignees** shows who has it.
