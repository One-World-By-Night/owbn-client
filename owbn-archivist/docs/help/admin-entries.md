# All Entries

Every request in OAT, whoever filed it and wherever it stands, for the Archivist's team.

## Who can use this

Accounts with the Archivist's access on the Archivist site: site admins, and anyone a site admin has given it.

## How to get there

On archivist.owbn.net, wp-admin → **Archivist** → **All Entries**.

## The screen

- **All Domains** and **All Statuses** - narrow the list.
- **The table** - **ID**, **Domain**, **Status**, **Step**, **Submitted By**, **Chronicle** and **Date**. Click a request to open it.

## Common tasks

### Find a request

1. Pick its domain and status, or sort by **Date** or **Chronicle**.
2. Click it to open its page.

## Things to know

- Requests are records and stay as filed: to stop one, deny it, or its filer withdraws it, on its page.
- Members follow their own requests on the [Archivist Inbox](inbox.md); this list is the whole archive.

## Troubleshooting

- **The menu isn't there.** Your account doesn't have the Archivist's access on the Archivist site. A site admin gives it.
