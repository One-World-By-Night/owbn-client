# Governance Records

Records a governance event, such as an election result or an interim appointment.

## Who can use this

Filed by: Usually an exec office or the Archivist.

## How to get there

On the website: the Archivist Submit page → Domain **Governance Records** → Form **Governance Records**. In wp-admin: Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reporter | Yes | Type it in. |
| Details of governance record | Yes | Formatted text. |
| Associated User | - | e.g., interim coordinator appointee |
| Associated Vote Link | - | e.g., election result |
| Record Type | Yes | Pick one. Choices: Interim Appointment, Election Result, Policy Change, Organizational Record, Other. |

The form fills in **Submitted By** and your email from your account.

Reviewers fill in their own fields on the request's page: **Review Comments**, **Escalation Reason**, **Resolution Notes**, **Resolution Type**.

### Where it goes

Submission → Archivist Review.

## Common tasks

### File it

1. Open Submit a Request and pick **Governance Records**, then **Governance Records**.
2. Fill in the fields above; the form stars the ones marked Yes.
3. Click **Submit Request** and follow it from its page.

## Things to know

- It goes straight to the Archivist.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty. Fill in every field marked Yes above.
