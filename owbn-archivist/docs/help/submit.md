# Submit a Request

Where you file a request with the Archivist: R&U, custom content, a character's registration, transfer or death, a chronicle report, a disciplinary action, and the rest.

## Who can use this

Anyone signed in. Which characters you can pick depends on who you are: a player picks their own characters, staff pick their chronicle's, and Coordinators and the Archivist pick from every character. Players, chronicle staff (HST, ST, CM and AST), Coordinators, the exec offices and site admins can create a character here: staff in their own chronicles, a player only their own PC, in any chronicle. See [Who Can Do What](roles.md).

## How to get there

- **On the website:** the Archivist Submit page.
- **In wp-admin:** Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

- **Domain** - the kind of request: Character Lifecycle, Custom Content, Chronicle Actions, Binding Agreements, Disciplinary Actions, Governance Records or Player Actions.
- **Form** - the request itself, when the domain has more than one. The form's fields load below it.
- **The form's fields** - they differ from form to form; each form has its own help page, listed in [Related](#related).
- **Character Name** (on forms about a character) - search for the character by name; **(clear)** starts over.
- **Create New Character** (players, chronicle staff, Coordinators, the exec offices and site admins) - for a character the registry doesn't have yet. It opens a panel:
  - **Character Name**, **Home Chronicle** and **PC / NPC**, all required. **Home Chronicle** is a search box: type a few letters, then click the chronicle in the list. Players are offered every chronicle still running; staff who aren't players, their own. A player's panel has no PC / NPC choice: it makes their own PC, under their name and email. In a chronicle you aren't staff of you can only create your own PC, and its HST approves it when you file the Registration.
  - **Coordinator Office (owns this NPC)** - Coordinators only; pick your office to register an NPC it owns, with no chronicle.
  - **Genre**, **Faction**, **Creature Type** and **Variant**.
  - **Player Name** and **Player Email** - required for a PC: the player who plays it, and the email of their OWbN account. **This is my character** fills in your own name and email.
  - **Create Character** saves the character and picks it for the request; **Cancel** closes the panel.
- **Submitting As** or **Your Role** (some forms) - whether you file as a player, staff, Coordinator or the Archivist. It decides whose characters the character search offers.
- **Submit Request** - files the request. It reads "Submitting…" while it sends.

When the request is filed, its own page opens, where you can follow it. It starts with a green box, **Request #N submitted**, and a button, **Submit another** with the form's name (for example **Submit another R&U Request**), that opens this page again on the same form. A form that files several requests at once lists each new request instead.

## Common tasks

### File a request for an existing character

1. Pick the **Domain**, then the **Form**.
2. In **Character Name**, type part of the name and pick the character.
3. Fill in the form's fields.
4. Click **Submit Request**.

### File several requests of the same kind in a row

1. File the first one with **Submit Request**.
2. On the page that opens, click **Submit another R&U Request** (the button carries the name of the form you just used).
3. The submit page opens with the Domain and the Form already chosen. Fill in the next request and click **Submit Request**.

### File a request for a character the registry doesn't have (staff)

1. Pick the Domain and the Form.
2. Click **Create New Character**.
3. Fill in Character Name, Home Chronicle, the creature lists and **PC / NPC**.
4. For a PC, fill in the player's **Player Name** and **Player Email**. If they have an OWbN account, the character is linked to it now; if not, it links itself the first time their account reaches the Archivist site.
5. Click **Create Character**, then finish the form and click **Submit Request**.

### Register your own new PC (player)

1. Pick the Domain and the Form.
2. Click **Create New Character**.
3. Fill in Character Name, pick the chronicle you play in (or want to join) in Home Chronicle, and fill in the creature lists.
4. Click **Create Character**, then finish the form and click **Submit Request**.

### Register an NPC your office owns (Coordinator)

1. Click **Create New Character**.
2. Pick your office in **Coordinator Office (owns this NPC)** and leave Home Chronicle empty.
3. Fill in the rest and click **Create Character**.

## Things to know

- A new PC is linked to its player's account, never to the person who created it, unless you tick **This is my character**.
- Search before you create: creating a character that already exists makes a second record. If that happens, tell the Archivist.
- A filed request goes through its form's review steps; see [How a Request Moves](approval-flow.md).
- The green **Request submitted** box shows only to the person who filed the request, right after they file it. Opening the request later doesn't show it.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Please log in to submit a request."** Sign in first.
- **"Please select a domain."** Pick a Domain before you submit.
- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty or something in the form was refused; the message under the form says which when it can. The refused request stays under My Submissions as Cancelled: fix the form and submit again.
- **"A PC needs its player's name and email."** Fill in Player Name and Player Email, or tick This is my character if the PC is yours.
- **"That player email doesn't look right."** Check the email for a typo.
- **"Choose a Home Chronicle, or select a Coordinator Office for an office NPC."** Pick the character's chronicle; an office NPC needs your office instead.
- **"..." is not one of the chronicles in your list.** You typed a chronicle but didn't click it. Type a few letters and click the chronicle in the list that opens. If it never appears, ask that chronicle's HST or staff to add you.
- **"You do not have permission to create characters."** Players, chronicle staff, Coordinators, the exec offices and site admins create characters. If your role hasn't reached OAT yet, sign out and back in.
- **"You can only create characters in a chronicle you belong to."** Your account holds no player role and no role on that chronicle's staff. Players create their own PC in any chronicle; staff create in their own. If your role hasn't reached OAT yet, sign out and back in.
- **"Only staff can create NPCs."** Players create their own PCs; ask your HST for an NPC.
- **"You can't file this for that character. Pick one of your own, or one in a chronicle you are staff of."** The request named a character you may not file for: a player files for their own characters, staff for their chronicle's, and Coordinators and the Archivist for any. Pick the character from the search.
- **The character I need isn't in the search.** It isn't one you can file for: a player only sees their own characters and staff only their chronicle's. Its HST or a Coordinator can file for it.
