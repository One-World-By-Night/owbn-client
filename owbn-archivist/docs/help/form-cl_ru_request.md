# R&U Request

Records a character's regulated items: the Regulation & Usage rules it falls under, and the Coordinators who control them.

## Who can use this

Filed by: Players for their own characters, and staff for their chronicle's.

## How to get there

On the website: the Archivist Submit page → Domain **Character Lifecycle** → Form **R&U Request**. In wp-admin: Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chronicle | Yes | Search for the chronicle. |
| Character Name | Yes | Search for an existing character or create a new one. |
| Character Status | - | Pick one. Choices: Active, Dead, Inactive, Removed IC, Removed OOC. |
| Regulation Rules | - | Select applicable regulation rules. |
| Controlling Coordinator(s) | - | Automatically populated from selected regulation rules. |
| Coordinators Who Must Approve | - | Tick every coordinator whose power, rule or item this uses (for example necromancy: the Giovanni Coordinator). Each one you tick must approve. A rule that doesn't say which coordinator also needs this; with nothing ticked it goes to the character's clan or sect coordinator. |

The form fills in **Submitted By** and your email from your account.

Reviewers fill in their own fields on the request's page: **Review Note**, **Coordinator Note**, **Resolution Notes**, **Resolution Type**.

### Where it goes

Submission → Staff Review (the chronicle's HST) → Coordinator Review, when a rule needs a Coordinator → Archivist Review. When you are the Coordinator of an office the request belongs to, Staff Review is skipped and it goes straight to Coordinator Review.

## Common tasks

### File it

1. Open Submit a Request and pick **Character Lifecycle**, then **R&U Request**.
2. Fill in the fields above; the form stars the ones marked Yes.
3. Click **Submit Request** and follow it from its page.

## Things to know

- Pick every rule the item falls under; **Controlling Coordinator(s)** fills in from them and decides who reviews it.
- A rule marked Elevated means a Coordinator must act; the request won't approve itself.
- Tick every coordinator whose power, rule or item the request uses under **Coordinators Who Must Approve**; each one must approve before it moves on. A rule that doesn't say which coordinator (such as "Appropriate Coordinator") goes to the character's clan or sect coordinator unless you tick one.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty. Fill in every field marked Yes above.
