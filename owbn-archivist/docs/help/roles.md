# Who Can Do What

What each OWbN role can see and do in the Archivist Toolkit (OAT), on every OWbN site.

## Who can use this

Everyone. Your roles come from your OWbN account, and every OWbN site reads the same ones.

## How to get there

This is a reference page; the other OAT help pages link here. Your roles are set on your OWbN account at sso.owbn.net: your HST, your Coordinator or the Web team changes them.

## The roles

| Role | In OAT |
| --- | --- |
| Player (any signed-in member) | Submit requests for your own characters, and create your own PC in any chronicle while submitting; that chronicle's HST approves it. See your linked characters under My Characters. Retire your own PC by changing its Status |
| HST | Everything a player can. Submit requests as staff for your chronicle's characters, and create a character in your chronicle while submitting. See your chronicle's characters and change every field on them, the linked account and the Sect included. Add grants. Review the requests a workflow step gives your chronicle's staff. See your chronicle's reports |
| Staff (ST) or CM | The same as the HST, except reviews: workflow steps go to the HST, and to the CM on Player Actions |
| AST | Everything a player can. Submit requests as staff for your chronicle's characters. See your chronicle's characters and change every field on them, the linked account and the Sect included. Add grants. Create characters in your chronicle. No reviews and no reports |
| Coordinator | Review the items a workflow step gives your office. See the characters granted to your office, starting with the items your office owns (Show All adds the rest). Change every field on your office's NPCs. Create characters and office NPCs. See your office's reports |
| Sub-Coordinator | The same as the Coordinator, except creating characters |
| Archivist (and the Archivist's Sub-Coordinator) | See every character and change every field on it. Revoke grants. Act on the Archivist steps of requests. See every report |
| AHC1, AHC2, Web Coordinator | See every character and change every field on it. Revoke grants. See every report on the Archivist site's Details page |
| Head Coordinator | See every character, and every report on the Archivist site's Details page. Act on the steps a workflow gives the Head Coordinator, such as global DAs. Characters stay read-only |
| Archivist staff | See every character and every report, without changing them |
| Membership Coordinator | See every report |
| Site admin | Everything, including OAT's own setup on the Archivist site: domains, forms, fields, workflow steps, rules, the creature taxonomy and settings |

## Common tasks

### Find out what you can do on a character

1. Open the [Character Record](registry-detail.md). The fields you can change show as boxes and lists; everything else shows as text.

### Get a role added or changed

1. Ask your HST for a chronicle role, your Coordinator for an office role, or the Web team for anything else.

## Things to know

- The same rules apply on archivist.owbn.net and on every other OWbN site, in wp-admin and on the website.
- Searching OWbN accounts (to link a character, reassign or delegate a request, or fill a player field on a form) is open to staff, Coordinators, the Archivist and site admins, not to players.
- A Coordinator's office gets access to a character when an item the office owns is approved on it.
- A request's page opens only for the people it involves: who filed it, who has had it, its chronicle's staff, its Coordinator offices, its character's player and the Archivist. Disciplinary Actions are open to every HST, CM, staff member, Coordinator and exec officer. See [A Request's Page](entry.md#who-can-use-this).
- A change to your roles reaches OAT within about fifteen minutes, or at once when you sign in again.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You do not have permission to create characters."** Players, chronicle staff, Coordinators and the exec offices create characters; Sub-Coordinators don't. If your role hasn't reached OAT yet, sign out and back in.
- **"You do not have permission to view this character."** The character isn't linked to you and isn't granted to your chronicle or office.
- **A tab or section you expect is empty.** Your account may not hold the role yet. Sign out and back in; if it is still empty, ask whoever gives that role.
