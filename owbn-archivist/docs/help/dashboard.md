# Archivist Dashboard

Where the Archivist pages start: what waits on you, what changed on the requests you watch, how many you have filed, and how you want Archivist emails.

## Who can use this

Anyone signed in.

## How to get there

- **On the website:** the Archivist Dashboard page.
- **In wp-admin:** the **Archivist** menu.

## The screen

- **Waiting on you** - up to five requests at a step you have, each linking to the request.
- **Changed this week on requests you watch** - up to five requests you watch that moved in the last seven days, newest first.
- **Archivist emails** - on the Archivist site, **One for each update** or **One a day, as a summary**, then **Save**. On other OWBN sites it is a link, **Archivist email settings**, to this choice on the Archivist.
- **Pending Actions** - requests at a step you have.
- **My Submissions** - requests you filed.
- **Watching** - requests you follow.
- **View Inbox** opens the [Archivist Inbox](inbox.md), **New Submission** opens [Submit a Request](submit.md), and **Registry** opens the [Archivist Registry](registry.md).

## Common tasks

### See what needs you

1. Look at **Pending Actions**, then click **View Inbox**.

### File a new request

1. Click **New Submission**.

### Get one email a day

1. Under **Archivist emails**, pick **One a day, as a summary**.
2. Click **Save**.

## Things to know

- Pending Actions counts only requests at your step; it is the number to watch. You also get an email when a request reaches you.
- You get an email when a request you watch moves to a new step, is sent back, or is approved, denied or cancelled. You never get the same news twice for one action.
- The summary goes out once a day at 13:00 UTC and holds every Archivist email since the last one. Switching back to **One for each update** sends what is waiting at once.
- The same lists are the **Archivist alerts** tile on My Dashboard (the **Comms** tab).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Please log in to view your dashboard."** Sign in first.
- **Pending Actions is 0 but someone says a request waits on you.** The request may be at a step for a role your account doesn't hold yet. Open it and check **Assignees**.
