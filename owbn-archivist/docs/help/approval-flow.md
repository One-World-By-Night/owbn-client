# How a Request Moves

The steps a request goes through after you file it, who has each step, and what happens when it is approved.

## Who can use this

Everyone who files or reviews requests. This page explains what the **Step:** line and the Timeline on [A Request's Page](entry.md) mean.

## How to get there

This is a reference page. Each request's own page shows the step it is at, who has it, and its history.

## The steps

| Step | Who has it | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submission | You, the filer | Where every request starts, and where it comes back when it is sent back to you |
| Staff Review | The HST of the character's chronicle; when it has no HST, its CM, and when it has no CM, its Staff (for Player Actions, the HST or the CM) | On most requests; skipped on Custom Content and R&U Requests filed by the Coordinator of the office they belong to |
| Coordinator Review | The Coordinator of the office the item belongs to | When the item needs a Coordinator's approval; a request can need several, and each one approves before it moves on |
| Gaining Chronicle Review | The HST of the chronicle a character is moving to | Transfers |
| Gaining Coordinator Review | The Coordinator of the office a character is moving to | Transfers to a Coordinator office |
| Membership Review | The Membership Coordinator and their staff | Creating, decommissioning or changing the standing of a chronicle |
| Executive Review, then Council Vote | The Head Coordinator | Global disciplinary actions |
| Staff Comment & Signature, then Player Comment & Signature | The HST, then the player | Binding agreements |
| Archivist Review | The Archivist | The last step of every request |

A step that doesn't apply to a request is skipped. Each domain runs these steps in order:

| Domain | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Character Lifecycle | Submission → Staff Review → Gaining Chronicle Review or Gaining Coordinator Review (transfers) → Coordinator Review → Archivist Review |
| Custom Content | Submission → Staff Review → Coordinator Review → Archivist Review |
| Chronicle Actions | Submission → Membership Review → Archivist Review |
| Disciplinary Actions | Submission → Staff Review (chronicle DAs) → Executive Review → Council Vote (global DAs) → Archivist Review |
| Binding Agreements | Submission → Staff Comment & Signature → Player Comment & Signature → Staff Review → Coordinator Review → Archivist Review |
| Governance Records | Submission → Archivist Review |
| Player Actions | Submission → Staff Review → Coordinator Review (genre registrations) → Archivist Review |

## Common tasks

### Follow your request

1. Open it from your Archivist Inbox or Dashboard.
2. **Step:** says where it is, **Assignees** says who has it, and the **Timeline** (for its staff and up) shows what has happened.

### Find what is waiting on you

1. Open your [Archivist Inbox](inbox.md). Requests at a step you have come first.

### Move a timed Coordinator Review along

1. Open the request and click **Bump**. A Coordinator Review approves itself after 14 days once it has had two bumps, unless a rule on it is Elevated.

## Things to know

- **When a request reaches a step, everyone assigned to that step gets an email** with a link that signs them in and opens the request. Whoever moved it there isn't emailed. Your Inbox still lists everything waiting on you.
- **Position mail goes to the position, by what the request is about.** Work for a Coordinator office goes to that office's address (for example, tremere@owbn.net). Work for a chronicle goes to its ST email: the HST's address on the chronicle's record, or the CM's {chronicle}-cm@owbn.net address. Work on your own character goes to your account email. The same rule decides where the result of something you filed goes: filed as a Coordinator or as Staff, or about something in your office or chronicle, it goes to that position's address. The subject names the position, so the mailbox keeps its history when the person changes. A position with no address on its record is emailed at the person's account email.
- **Lines filed together move together.** Several rules on one R&U Request make several lines. Lines with the same approvers are grouped: the person reviewing gets one email for the group, can approve every line with one click, and can veto a single line. The filer gets one email when lines are approved and one when a line is vetoed.
- **A Coordinator can bring in another.** At Coordinator Review, **Add a Coordinator** asks one more office to approve, and the request waits for them too. The Archivist can do the same before its own step. When nobody holds the office yet, the Timeline says so and the Archivist can approve for it.
- When a request is approved or denied, the person who filed it gets an email; a denial carries the reason. When it is sent back, whoever has it next gets an email.
- An approved request shows under **Registry Entries** on the [Character Record](registry-detail.md). When the item belongs to a Coordinator office, the office gets access to the character.
- **Hold** stops a request and its timer; **Resume** starts the timer again where it stopped.
- A regulation rule marked **Elevated** stops a timed step from approving itself; a person has to act.
- The Timeline shows each person what their part allows: the request's staff see staff entries, Coordinators see theirs and staff's, and the Archivist sees everything. Players don't see the Timeline.
- A send-back goes to the step before, passing over any step that didn't apply to the request.

## Troubleshooting

- **My request has sat at a step for days.** Check **Assignees** on its page and ask them, or ask the Archivist.
- **It skipped a step I expected.** That step doesn't apply to this request; for example, Coordinator Review only runs when the item needs a Coordinator.
- **It came back to Submission.** Someone clicked Request Changes. Their note, in the **Changes Requested** box at the top of the request and in your email, says what to change; fix it and click **Resubmit**.
