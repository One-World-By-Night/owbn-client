# Player Actions

Asks to join a chronicle, or to register with a Coordinator office.

## Who can use this

Filed by: Any player.

## How to get there

On the website: the Archivist Submit page → Domain **Player Actions** → Form **Player Actions**. In wp-admin: Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action | Yes | What would you like to do? Choices: Join a Chronicle, Genre Register. |
| Chronicle | Yes | Select the chronicle you want to join. |
| Coordinator Office | Yes | Select the coordinator office to register with. |
| Notes | - | Type it in. |

The form fills in **Submitted By** and your email from your account.

### Where it goes

Submission → Staff Review (the chronicle's HST or CM) → Coordinator Review, for registering with a Coordinator office → Archivist Review.

## Common tasks

### File it

1. Open Submit a Request and pick **Player Actions**, then **Player Actions**.
2. Fill in the fields above; the form stars the ones marked Yes.
3. Click **Submit Request** and follow it from its page.

## Things to know

- The chronicle's HST or CM reviews it first.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty. Fill in every field marked Yes above.
