# Creature Taxonomy

The lists behind every Creature picker in OAT: genres, the factions in each, the types in each faction, and their variants.

## Who can use this

Accounts with the Archivist's access on the Archivist site. A change here reaches every creature picker on every OWbN site within the hour.

## How to get there

On archivist.owbn.net, wp-admin → **Archivist** → **Settings** → **Creature Taxonomy** tab.

## The screen

- **Creature Type Taxonomy** - the tree: each genre, its factions, each faction's types, and each type's variants. Add, rename or remove an entry at any level.
- **Save Changes** and **Cancel**.
- **Export JSON** saves the whole tree to a file; **Import JSON** loads one back.

## Common tasks

### Add a clan to a Sect's list

1. Open the Vampire genre and the Sect's faction.
2. Add the type, and its variants if it has any.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

### Back up the lists before a big change

1. Click **Export JSON** and keep the file.

## Things to know

- For Vampire, a faction's types are what that Sect may take at character creation, as the Character Bylaws list them. A character outside that list is a Sect Defector; the [Character Record](registry-detail.md) keeps such a clan and marks it.
- Renaming an entry doesn't change characters that already use the old name; their record keeps it and shows it as "(not on this list)".
- Other OWbN sites read the lists through the Archivist site and keep a copy for up to an hour.

## Troubleshooting

- **A new type doesn't show on another site.** That site still has its copy from before; it refreshes within the hour, or the Web team can clear it.
