# Fame Registry

The public list of famous characters across OWbN: who they are known as, their fame level, and where they come from.

## Who can use this

Anyone, signed in or not. It shows only what the Fame Registry form records as public.

## How to get there

- **On the website:** the Fame Registry page on archivist.owbn.net or council.owbn.net.

## The screen

- **Filter by name, chronicle, influence...** - narrows the list as you type, with a count of how many records match "of" the total.
- **The table** - **Character**, **Home Chronicle** and **Level**, sortable by clicking a header. A row opens to the character's public **Identity:**, **Influences:** and **Notes:**.

## Common tasks

### Look up a famous character

1. Type part of the name, the chronicle or an influence into the filter.
2. Click the row for the details.

### Put a character on the registry

1. File the [Fame Registry](form-cl_fame_registry.md) form for the character. Once it is approved, the character shows here.

## Things to know

- The registry shows the public identity the form records, which can differ from the character's real name.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No fame records found."** Nothing matches the filter; clear it.
- **A character I registered isn't here.** Its Fame Registry request isn't approved yet; check it in your [Archivist Inbox](inbox.md).
