# OAT Troubleshooting

The problems people hit most in the Archivist Toolkit, and where each is answered.

## Who can use this

Everyone.

## How to get there

This is a reference page. Each OAT help page also ends with its own troubleshooting.

## The problems

| Problem | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| My character isn't under My Characters | It isn't linked to your account. Use Connect a character on the [Registry](registry.md), or see [Character Record](registry-detail.md#troubleshooting) |
| A character I registered shows as someone else's | Its Linked Account is wrong. Change it on its [Character Record](registry-detail.md#common-tasks) |
| "You do not have permission to view this character." | It isn't yours or your chronicle's. See [Who Can Do What](roles.md) |
| I can't change a character's Sect without losing the clan | Pick the new Faction; the clan stays, marked "(Sect Defector)". See [Character Record](registry-detail.md#common-tasks) |
| My request has sat at a step for days | The people assigned are emailed when it reaches them, but an email can be missed, and a step with nobody assigned sends none. See [How a Request Moves](approval-flow.md#troubleshooting) |
| I have no buttons on a request | The current step isn't yours. See [A Request's Page](entry.md#troubleshooting) |
| "You do not have access to this request." | The request isn't part of your work. See [A Request's Page](entry.md#who-can-use-this) |
| "You do not have permission to create characters." | Players, chronicle staff, Coordinators and the exec offices create them: staff in their own chronicles, a player only their own PC. See [Submit a Request](submit.md#troubleshooting) |
| "A PC needs its player's name and email." | Fill in the player's name and email. See [Submit a Request](submit.md#troubleshooting) |
| "Submission failed. Please check the form and try again." | A required field is empty, or something in the form was refused; a refused request stays under My Submissions as Cancelled. See the form's own page from [Submit a Request](submit.md#related) |
| "No account matches that." | Nobody has an account with that name, login or email address yet, or only part of an address was typed. See [Character Record](registry-detail.md#troubleshooting) |
| A report shows only part of OWbN | It shows what your roles reach. See [Reports](reports.md) |

## Common tasks

### Get help with something not listed

1. Open a ticket at support.owbn.net, with the request's number or the character's name.

## Things to know

- Most "I can't see it" problems come down to roles or the character's linked account; [Who Can Do What](roles.md) explains both.

## Troubleshooting

- **A new role doesn't seem to work.** Roles reach OAT within about fifteen minutes, or at once when you sign in again.
