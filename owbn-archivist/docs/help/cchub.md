# ccHub

The Custom Content Database: every piece of approved custom content in OWbN, by category, open to everyone.

## Who can use this

Anyone, signed in or not.

## How to get there

- **On the website:** the ccHub pages on archivist.owbn.net, chronicles, council, players, support and sso.

## The screen

- **Custom Content Database** - a card per category with its count, and **All Custom Content**. Click a card to browse it.
- **The browse table** - **Name**, **Tradition**, **XP** and **Coordinator**, a page at a time; **Next »** goes on.
- **An item** - click its name to read it in a pop-up; **Open in new tab** opens it on its own page.

## Common tasks

### Find a piece of custom content

1. Click its category, or **All Custom Content**.
2. Page through, and click the name.

### Add custom content

1. File the [Custom Content](form-custom_content.md) form. Once it is approved, it shows here.

## Things to know

- Only approved custom content shows; a request still in review doesn't.
- A character who learns custom content files [Learn Custom Content](form-cl_learn_custom_content.md), which points at the item here.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No custom content found."** The category is empty.
