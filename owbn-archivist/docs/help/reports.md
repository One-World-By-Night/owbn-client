# Reports

Counts and lists drawn from the registry and the requests: chronicle reports, entries by domain and status, custom content, disciplinary actions, and character totals.

## Who can use this

HSTs, staff and CMs see their own chronicles; Coordinators and Sub-Coordinators see their own office. Everything is open to:

| Report screen | Everything for |
| --- | --- |
| OAT Reports (every OWbN site) | Site admins, the Archivist and the Archivist's staff, the Membership Coordinator |
| Details (the Archivist site) | The same, plus the Head Coordinator, the AHCs, the Web Coordinator and the Admin office |

Players and ASTs have no reports.

## How to get there

- **OAT Reports:** on archivist.owbn.net, wp-admin → **Archivist** → **Reports**. The other OWbN sites don't have it.
- **Details:** on archivist.owbn.net, wp-admin → **Archivist** → **Details**. If your menu doesn't show it, open archivist.owbn.net/wp-admin/admin.php?page=oat-reports.

## The screen

**OAT Reports** has a tab per report:

- **Chronicle Reports** - one row per chronicle, labelled "City, State, Country: Name"; click a row for its reports, newest first. A filter box matches name, city, state and region.
- **Pending Reports** - chronicle reports nobody has decided yet, oldest first, each marked "Awaiting archivist" or "Sent back to submitter".
- **Entries by Domain**, **Entries by Status** and **Entries by Coordinator** - request counts.
- **Custom Content by Type** - approved custom content, by kind.
- **Disciplinary Actions** and **DA by Level** - disciplinary actions, and how many at each level.
- **Recent Activity** - the latest requests and actions.

**Details** has a tab per list: **All Characters**, **Active Only**, **By Region**, **By Chronicle**, **By Player**, **By Creature Type** and **By R&U Classification**. Each tab exports to CSV. A Coordinator sees the items their office owns; **Show All** adds the rest on the same characters.

## Common tasks

### Find a chronicle's reports

1. Open **OAT Reports** → **Chronicle Reports**.
2. Type part of the chronicle's name, city or state into the filter, and click its row.

### See which chronicle reports still wait

1. Open **Pending Reports**.

### Export a list

1. Open **Details** and the tab you want.
2. Click **Export CSV**.

## Things to know

- The two screens draw on the same requests; Details lists characters and R&U items, and OAT Reports counts requests.
- Your reach on each screen comes from your roles, so a new role shows within about fifteen minutes or at your next sign-in.
- A Coordinator also sees, under Chronicle Reports, every report that ticked their office in **Notify Coordinators**; the email those reports send links here.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You do not have permission to view reports."** Reports are for staff, Coordinators and the exec offices.
- **A report shows only part of OWbN.** It shows what your role reaches; see [Who can use this](#who-can-use-this).
