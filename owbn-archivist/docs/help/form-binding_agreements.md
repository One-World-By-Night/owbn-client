# Binding Agreements

A binding agreement between a player and a chronicle or a Coordinator office, with both sides' comments and signatures.

## Who can use this

Filed by: The player, the chronicle's staff, or a Coordinator; **Your Role** says which.

## How to get there

On the website: the Archivist Submit page → Domain **Binding Agreements** → Form **Binding Agreements**. In wp-admin: Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Role | Yes | How are you submitting this agreement? Choices: Player, Staff (HST / CM), Coordinator. |
| Player this agreement is for | Yes | Search for the player. If not found, type their name. |
| Chronicle | Yes | Select the chronicle this agreement is associated with. |
| Coordinator Genre | Yes | Select the coordinator genre this agreement falls under. |
| Agreement Template | - | Select a template to pre-fill the agreement text. You can edit after selection. |
| Agreement Text | Yes | Pre-canned template, editable by submitter. |
| Specific terms or scope of this agreement | Yes | Type it in. |
| Player Signature | Yes | Type your name to sign. |
| Chronicle Representative Signature | Yes | Type your name to sign. |
| Coordinator Signature (if applicable) | - | Type your name to sign. |

The form fills in **Submitted By** and your email from your account.

Reviewers fill in their own fields on the request's page: **Review Comments**, **Player Signature**, **Chronicle Representative Signature**, **Coordinator Signature**, **Escalation Reason**, **Resolution Notes**, **Resolution Type**.

### Where it goes

Submission → Staff Comment & Signature (when a Coordinator files it) → Player Comment & Signature (when staff or a Coordinator files it) → Staff Review → Coordinator Review → Archivist Review.

## Common tasks

### File it

1. Open Submit a Request and pick **Binding Agreements**, then **Binding Agreements**.
2. Fill in the fields above; the form stars the ones marked Yes.
3. Click **Submit Request** and follow it from its page.

## Things to know

- The other side comments and signs on the request's page before it goes to review.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty. Fill in every field marked Yes above.
