# Transfer

Moves a character to another chronicle, or to a Coordinator office.

## Who can use this

Filed by: The character's player, or its chronicle's staff.

## How to get there

On the website: the Archivist Submit page → Domain **Character Lifecycle** → Form **Transfer**. In wp-admin: Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chronicle | Yes | Search for the chronicle. |
| Character Name | Yes | Search for an existing character or create a new one. |
| Character Status | - | Pick one. Choices: Active, Dead, Inactive, Removed IC, Removed OOC. |
| Transfer To | Yes | Select the chronicle or coordinator office receiving this character. |

The form fills in **Submitted By** and your email from your account.

Reviewers fill in their own fields on the request's page: **Review Note**, **Coordinator Note**, **Resolution Notes**, **Resolution Type**.

### Where it goes

Submission → Staff Review (the chronicle's HST) → Gaining Chronicle Review (the receiving chronicle's HST), or Gaining Coordinator Review (the receiving office's Coordinator) for a move to a Coordinator office → Coordinator Review, when a rule needs a Coordinator → Archivist Review.

## Common tasks

### File it

1. Open Submit a Request and pick **Character Lifecycle**, then **Transfer**.
2. Fill in the fields above; the form stars the ones marked Yes.
3. Click **Submit Request** and follow it from its page.

## Things to know

- The receiving chronicle's HST reviews it too, at Gaining Chronicle Review. A move to a Coordinator office goes to that office's Coordinator instead, at Gaining Coordinator Review.
- A transfer into a Coordinator office has no gaining chronicle to review it; ask the Archivist before you file one.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty. Fill in every field marked Yes above.
