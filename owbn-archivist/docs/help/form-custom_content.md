# Custom Content

Proposes a new piece of custom content, such as a combination discipline or a ritual, for approval and the ccHub.

## Who can use this

Filed by: Players, staff, Coordinators or the Archivist; **Submitting As** says which.

## How to get there

On the website: the Archivist Submit page → Domain **Custom Content** → Form **Custom Content**. In wp-admin: Archivist → Workspace → **New Submission** tab.

## The screen

| Field | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submitting As | Yes | Pick one. Choices: As Player, As Staff, As Coordinator, As Archivist. |
| Character | Yes | Search for an existing character or create a new one. |
| Coordinator Office | - | Optional. Leave blank to auto-assign from character creature type. |
| Name of Custom Content | Yes | Type it in. |
| Content Type | Yes | Options depend on creature type selected above. Choices: Combination Discipline / Technique, Blood Magic: Ritual, Blood Magic: Path, Discipline, Inceptor Discipline, Merit, Path of Enlightenment, Sabbat Ritae, Kuei-jin: Ritual, Fallen Rituals, …. |
| Blood Magic Category | - | Blood magic category (e.g. Thaumaturgy, Necromancy). Choices: Thaumaturgy - Hermetic, Thaumaturgy - Sielanic, Dark Thaumaturgy, Akhu, Assamite Sorcery - Dur-An-Ki, Koldunic Sorcery, Abyssal Mysticism, Natib Athirat, Sadhana, Black Hand Aljusuri, …. |
| Discipline Requirements | - | Prerequisite disciplines (one per line). |
| XP Cost | Yes | A number. |
| MET Rules / Mechanics | Yes | Full mechanical write-up of the custom content. |
| Chronicle Where Content Was Created | Yes | Search for the chronicle. |
| HST Who Approved | Yes | Auto-populated from chronicle selection when possible. |
| Date of Archival | - | A date. |
| Applicable R&U Classifications | - | Select any applicable Regulation & Usage rules. |

The form fills in **Submitted By** and your email from your account.

Reviewers fill in their own fields on the request's page: **Review Comments**, **Reason for Escalation**, **Resolution Notes**, **Resolution**.

### Where it goes

Submission → Staff Review (the chronicle's HST) → Coordinator Review (every Coordinator it needs) → Archivist Review. When you are the Coordinator of an office the content belongs to, Staff Review is skipped and it goes straight to Coordinator Review.

## Common tasks

### File it

1. Open Submit a Request and pick **Custom Content**, then **Custom Content**.
2. Fill in the fields above; the form stars the ones marked Yes.
3. Click **Submit Request** and follow it from its page.

## Things to know

- Each Coordinator the content falls under approves it; it shows on the [ccHub](cchub.md) once the Archivist approves.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Submission failed. Please check the form and try again."** A required field is empty. Fill in every field marked Yes above.
