# Archivist Registry

Every character you can see in the registry, grouped by chronicle or Coordinator office, with a search across all of them.

## Who can use this

Anyone signed in. What each tab holds depends on who you are:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| My Characters | The PCs linked to your account |
| Chronicles | Your own chronicles, if you are their HST, AST, staff or CM; every open chronicle for the Archivist, the AHCs, the Head Coordinator, the Web Coordinator and site admins |
| Coordinators | Your own office, if you are its Coordinator or Sub-Coordinator; every office for the Archivist and the rest above |
| Decommissioned | Closed chronicles, by the same rule as Chronicles |

## How to get there

- **On the website:** the Archivist Registry page.
- **In wp-admin:** Archivist → Workspace → **Registry** tab.

## The screen

- **Display Options** - tick the columns you want: Player, Type, PC/NPC, Entries, My Entries and Last Activity. Character, Chronicle and Status are always shown. Your choice is kept for your account.
- **The tabs** - My Characters, Chronicles, Coordinators and Decommissioned.
- **Search characters...** - finds characters by name or chronicle across everything you can see, from two characters on. **Clear** empties the search and every column filter.
- **The sections** - one per chronicle or Coordinator office, each with its count. Click a section to open it.
- **The table**, in each open section:

  | Column | Shows |
  | --- | --- |
  | Character | The name; click it to open the [Character Record](registry-detail.md) in a new tab |
  | Player | The player's name on the record |
  | Chronicle | The home chronicle |
  | Type | The creature type |
  | PC/NPC | PC or NPC |
  | Status | Active, inactive, dead or shelved |
  | Entries | Approved requests on the character |
  | My Entries | Approved requests on it that your office owns |
  | Last Activity | The date of its latest approved request |

  Under the headers, a **Filter...** box per column narrows the rows as you type. Click a header to sort by it; click again to reverse.

## Common tasks

### Find a character

1. Type at least two letters of its name, or its chronicle, into **Search characters...**.
2. Click the name to open its record.

### See a chronicle's or an office's characters

1. Open the **Chronicles** or **Coordinators** tab.
2. Click the chronicle or office.

### Choose your columns

1. Open **Display Options**.
2. Tick or untick the columns.

## Things to know

- A character shows under My Characters only when its record is linked to your account. A PC registered with your email links itself the first time your account reaches the Archivist site.
- A Coordinator sees a character once an item their office owns is approved on it, which grants the office access.
- Counts and Last Activity count approved requests only.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No sections found."** You have no chronicle or office for this tab. Players only have My Characters.
- **"No characters."** The section is empty.
- **"No characters found."** Nothing you can see matches the search. Check the spelling, or try the chronicle's name.
- **My character isn't under My Characters.** It isn't linked to your account. Ask its HST to link it on the [Character Record](registry-detail.md).
