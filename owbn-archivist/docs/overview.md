# OAT Overview

The Archivist Toolkit (OAT) is where OWbN keeps its records: every character in the registry, and every request about them, from registration and R&U to transfers, deaths, custom content, chronicle reports and disciplinary actions.

## What it does

- **Requests.** A member files a request on a form; it goes through review steps, staff, then Coordinators where needed, then the Archivist; and once approved it becomes part of the record.
- **The registry.** Every character has a record: its player, its chronicle, its creature, who can see it, and every approved request on it.
- **Reports.** Counts and lists for staff, Coordinators and the exec team.
- **Public pages.** The ccHub lists approved custom content, and the Fame Registry lists famous characters.

## Where it lives

OAT runs on archivist.owbn.net. Its pages, Dashboard, Inbox, Submit, Registry and the rest, also appear on chronicles, council, players, support and sso, and they all read and write the same records. Your OWbN account and your roles are the same on every site.

## Who does what

- **Players** file requests for their own characters and see them under My Characters.
- **HSTs and chronicle staff** file for their chronicle's characters, review requests at Staff Review, and keep their characters' records right.
- **Coordinators** review the items their office controls.
- **The Archivist** gives the final approval and keeps the records.

[Who Can Do What](help/roles.md) has the full list.
