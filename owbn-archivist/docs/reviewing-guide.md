# Reviewing Requests

How to find and act on the requests that reach you, for HSTs, chronicle staff, Coordinators and the Archivist.

## Finding your work

When a request reaches your step you get an email with a link that signs you in and opens it. Your work is also under **Assigned to Me** in your [Archivist Inbox](help/inbox.md), and **Pending Actions** on the Dashboard counts it. Check it often; an email can be missed.

A step comes to you by name or by role: Staff Review goes to the chronicle's HST (its CM when it has no HST, its Staff when it has no CM), Coordinator Review to the office's Coordinator, and Archivist Review to the Archivist. [How a Request Moves](help/approval-flow.md) lists every step for every domain.

## Reading a request

Open it and read, top to bottom:

- **Details** - what the filer sent.
- **Linked Regulation Rules** - what the item falls under, and whether a rule is Elevated.
- **Timeline** - what has happened so far, with each note.
- **Assignees** - who else has had it.

## Acting on it

Every action except Bump needs a note.

- **Approve** sends it to the next step, or closes it as approved at the last one.
- **Deny** closes it; the filer is emailed your note as the reason.
- **Request Changes** sends it back; whoever gets it next is emailed.
- **Reassign** hands the step to someone else; **Delegate** asks someone to look and brings it back to you.

[A Request's Page](help/entry.md) covers every button, and who gets which.

## Timers

Coordinator Review approves itself after 14 days once the request has had two bumps, unless one of its rules is Elevated. Hold stops the clock and Resume starts it again.

## For Coordinators

You see the characters granted to your office. On a character's record, Registry Entries starts with the items your office owns; **Show All** adds the rest. An approved item your office owns grants your office the character.

## What approval does

An approved request shows under Registry Entries on the [Character Record](help/registry-detail.md). When the item belongs to a Coordinator office, that office gets access to the character.
