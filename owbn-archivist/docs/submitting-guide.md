# Submitting a Request

How to file a request with the Archivist and see it through, for players and chronicle staff.

## Before you start

- Sign in with your OWbN account. Your roles decide which characters you can file for: your own as a player, your chronicle's as staff.
- Know which form you need. The [form pages](help/submit.md#related) say what each one is for: Registration puts a character in the registry, R&U Request records its regulated items, Transfer moves it, Death records its end, and so on.

## Filing

1. Open the Archivist Submit page, or New Submission in wp-admin.
2. Pick the **Domain**, then the **Form**.
3. Pick the character. If the registry doesn't have it yet and you are its HST, click **Create New Character** and fill in its name, chronicle, creature and, for a PC, the player's name and email.
4. Fill in the form. Starred fields are required. On R&U forms, pick every regulation rule the item falls under; the Controlling Coordinator fills in from them.
5. Click **Submit Request**. The request's own page opens.

[Submit a Request](help/submit.md) covers every control on the screen.

## After you file

- **Follow it** from your [Archivist Inbox](help/inbox.md), under My Submissions. Its page shows the step it is at and who has it.
- **If it comes back to you**, someone asked for changes. Read their note on the Timeline, fix what they asked, add a note and click **Resubmit**.
- **If you change your mind**, write a note and click **Cancel / Withdraw**.
- **When it is approved or denied**, you get an email. An approved request shows on the character's record under Registry Entries.

## When a request seems stuck

Reviewers aren't emailed when a request reaches them, so a request can sit until someone checks their Inbox. Open it, look at **Assignees**, and ask that person, or the Archivist. [How a Request Moves](help/approval-flow.md) shows every step.

## Registering a PC for a player

When you create a PC for one of your players, fill in **their** name and email, not yours. If they already have an OWbN account, the PC is linked to it at once; if not, it links itself the first time their account reaches the Archivist site. Tick **This is my character** only for your own PC.
