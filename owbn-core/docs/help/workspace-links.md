# Workspace Links

The three link cards at the top of every member's My Board: Resources, Bylaws and Voting.

## Who can use this

Site admins, and the Web and Admin offices (Coordinators and their staff). Anyone else sees "You do not have permission to edit workspace links."

## How to get there

wp-admin → **OWBN Core** → **Workspace Links**.

## The screen

- Three groups, **Resources**, **Bylaws** and **Voting**, each a list of links with a **Link label** and an address. **Remove** takes one out; **+ Add link** adds one.
- **Save Workspace Links** saves all three.

## Common tasks

### Add a link to a card

1. Under the card's group, click **+ Add link**.
2. Fill in the label and the address.
3. Click **Save Workspace Links**.

## Things to know

- Use the sign-in form for links to another OWbN site, `https://host/?auth=sso&redirect_uri=/path/`, so members arrive signed in.
- Each site keeps its own set: what you save here shows on this site's My Board only.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You do not have permission to edit workspace links."** Your account needs a Web or Admin office role, or site admin.
