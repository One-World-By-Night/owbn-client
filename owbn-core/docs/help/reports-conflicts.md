# Conflicts Report

Accounts that hold more than one chronicle staff position across active chronicles, worst first.

## Who can use this

Site admins.

## How to get there

wp-admin → **Reports** → **Conflicts**.

## The screen

- One row per account: **Account**, **Email**, the number of positions (**#**), the **Issues** and the **Positions**.
- Issues, most serious first: **Multiple HST** (HST of more than one chronicle), **HST+CM same chronicle**, **No/placeholder email** and **Overlap**.
- **Refresh Data** - reads the chronicles again.
- Bulk: **Acknowledge (dismiss)** the rows you tick. Acknowledged rows move to **Acknowledged**, where **Restore to active** brings them back.

## Common tasks

### Clear a known overlap from the list

1. Tick the row.
2. Choose **Acknowledge (dismiss)**.

## Things to know

- Acknowledging only hides the row from this report; the positions don't change.
- Staff positions are changed on the chronicle itself, in C&C Manager.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No overlapping staff assignments found."** Every active chronicle's staff are different people.
